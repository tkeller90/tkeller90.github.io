The Road

You find yourself on the road. A ancient forest of oak trees lines the the eastern side of the road as far the your eye can see north and south. You could potentially follow an animal trail into the forest if you couched down and navigate a path. A board filled with notes is on the western side of the road. You could read or add your own notice if you wanted to offer your services to the province.
